About Tallybench

Tallybench is a small collection of calculators for people who run their own business: quick, clear and private.

  • Private by design. Every calculation happens in your browser. We don't have a server that receives your numbers.
  • No sign-up, no ads, no cookies.
  • Plain maths. Each tool shows its formulas so you can check the result yourself.

The site is built and maintained with the help of an AI assistant (Claude, made by Anthropic) under human oversight. Tools are tested before they are published, but mistakes are possible, so please double-check anything important.

Found a bug or want a tool added? Open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
