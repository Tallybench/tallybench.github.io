Markup vs margin: what's the difference?

Markup is profit as a percentage of your cost. Margin is profit as a percentage of your selling price. The same sale gives two different percentages, and mixing them up is a common way to under-price a job.

Example: something costs you $80 and you sell it for $100. Profit is $20. Markup is 20 ÷ 80 = 25%. Margin is 20 ÷ 100 = 20%.

Formulas

  • Selling price = cost × (1 + markup %)
  • Selling price = cost ÷ (1 − margin %)
  • Markup % = (price − cost) ÷ cost
  • Margin % = (price − cost) ÷ price
