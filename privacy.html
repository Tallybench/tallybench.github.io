Privacy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

What we collect

The numbers you type. Nothing. The calculators run entirely in your web browser. The values you enter are not sent to us, stored on our side or saved on your device.

Visit counts. We count visits to the site using GoatCounter, a privacy-focused analytics service. It records which page was viewed and, in aggregate form, things like the referring website, your country (worked out from your IP address), your browser and operating system, screen width and language. According to GoatCounter, it does not store IP addresses or full user-agent strings, does not set cookies and does not use other tracking identifiers in your browser, and does not share this data with third parties. We use the counts only to see how many people use the site and which pages are useful. If you'd rather not be counted, add #toggle-goatcounter to the end of any page address on this site and load it once; your browser will then skip the counter on this site (it remembers this with a small setting in your browser, which is the only thing GoatCounter ever stores there, and only if you ask for it). See GoatCounter's privacy policy.

We don't use advertising or marketing trackers, and we don't ask for any personal information.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page. We don't receive or control those logs. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example if we add ads or accounts), we'll update this page first and say what changed.

Questions

Open an issue on the project's GitHub page or email tallybench.io@gmail.com. If you email us, we receive your email address and message, and we use them only to reply.
