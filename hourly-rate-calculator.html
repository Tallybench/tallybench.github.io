How it works

  • Revenue needed = (take-home income + business expenses) ÷ (1 − tax set-aside %)
  • Billable hours = (52 − weeks off) × working hours per week × billable %
  • Hourly rate = revenue needed ÷ billable hours

Most freelancers can only bill a portion of their working week, because finding clients, invoicing and admin take time too. If you leave that out, you'll set your rate too low.
