How it works

  • Profit per unit = selling price − cost per unit
  • Break-even units = fixed costs ÷ profit per unit, rounded up to a whole unit
  • Break-even revenue = break-even units × selling price
  • Units for a target profit = (fixed costs + target profit) ÷ profit per unit, rounded up

Fixed costs are the bills you pay whether or not you sell anything. Cost per unit is what each extra sale costs you: materials, payment fees, shipping, or the contractor you pay per job. Make sure the fixed-cost period (a month, a year) matches the period you want the answer for.

If your cost per unit isn't clear, the markup and margin calculator can help you price each unit, and the hourly rate calculator covers pricing your own time.

A simplified model: it assumes a single product at a constant price and constant cost per unit, and ignores taxes. Results are arithmetic only and are not financial, tax or legal advice.
